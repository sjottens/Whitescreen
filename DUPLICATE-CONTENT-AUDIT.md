## SECTION 3: COPYSCAPE INSTRUCTIONS

**How to Use Copyscape (copyscape.com)**:
1. Go to https://www.copyscape.com/
2. Click "Paste Text" tab
3. Copy all text from page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C on web page)
4. Paste into Copyscape text box
5. Click "Check for Plagiarism"
6. Review results:
   - If results show 0-2% match to other sites: **LOW DUPLICATE RISK** (Original content)
   - If results show 3-10% match: **MEDIUM DUPLICATE RISK** (Similar phrasing, may need rewrite)
   - If results show 10%+: **HIGH DUPLICATE RISK** (Likely copied or templated)

---

## SECTION 4: ALTERNATIVE: Google Search Duplicate Detection

**Manual Google Check** (if Copyscape unavailable):
1. Copy unique phrase from page (10-15 words)
2. Paste in Google with quotes: `"exact phrase"`
3. If Google shows 0 results: Likely unique
4. If Google shows 1 result (your page): Likely unique
5. If Google shows multiple results: Likely duplicate or similar content exists

**Example**:
- Copied phrase: "ASUS ROG gaming monitors consistently achieve higher refresh rates than competitors"
- Google search: `"ASUS ROG gaming monitors consistently achieve higher refresh rates than competitors"`
- If 10+ results appear: This exact phrase is common → Content may be templated
